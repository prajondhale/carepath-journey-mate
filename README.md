# CarePath Journey

## Project Overview

CarePath Journey is a **patient-centric healthcare platform** that helps patients and caregivers organize medical documents, track treatment progress, identify pending requirements, manage appointments, and stay informed through timely notifications. It brings these aspects together into a single, simple, and accessible healthcare journey.

---

## The Problem

Healthcare journeys can become difficult to manage when patients have medical documents, prescriptions, reports, referrals, appointments, and follow-ups spread across different hospitals and platforms.

Patients and caregivers may struggle to:

* Keep track of important medical documents
* Understand which treatment stages are completed or pending
* Identify missing documents or requirements
* Remember upcoming appointments and follow-ups
* Keep caregivers informed about the patient's progress
* Understand healthcare information due to language and digital-literacy barriers
* Know what action needs to be taken next

Most existing healthcare systems focus on individual hospital services or record storage rather than giving patients a **simple, unified view of their complete healthcare journey**.

---

## CarePath Journey

CarePath Journey is designed to bring the different parts of a patient's healthcare journey together in one place.

It helps patients understand **where they are in their treatment, what has been completed, what is pending, which documents are required, and what they need to do next**.

The platform also provides a dedicated caregiver experience so authorized caregivers can stay informed about treatment progress, pending requirements, appointments, and important alerts.

---

## Features

### 🩺 Treatment Journey Tracker

Provides a clear step-by-step view of the patient's treatment journey, including completed stages, pending actions, and requirement gaps.

### 📄 Health Document Management

Organizes important medical documents such as reports, prescriptions, and referral letters in one place.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Caregiver View

Allows authorized caregivers to monitor the patient's treatment progress, upcoming actions, documents, and alerts.

### 🔔 Smart Notifications

Highlights important updates such as upcoming appointments, missing documents, new reports, and other required actions.

### 🔐 Document Verification

Demonstrates a document verification workflow with integrity checks and verification status.

### 🌐 Multilingual Interface

Provides a multilingual experience with functional support for English, Hindi, and Marathi.

### 🎫 Support Tickets

Allows patients and caregivers to raise and track issues that require assistance.

### 📱 Responsive & Accessible Design

Designed to provide a simple and readable experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## Project Scope & Impact

CarePath Journey is designed to make healthcare navigation **simpler, more organized, and more understandable for patients and caregivers**.

Instead of patients having to manage multiple documents, appointments, pending requirements, and treatment steps separately, CarePath brings these elements together into a single journey.

The platform can help users:

* Understand their current treatment status
* Keep important healthcare documents organized
* Identify missing requirements before the next step
* Stay aware of appointments and follow-ups
* Keep authorized caregivers informed
* Reduce confusion caused by fragmented healthcare information
* Navigate the healthcare journey through a simpler and more accessible interface

The prototype is designed with the needs of patients and caregivers in mind, particularly in semi-urban and rural healthcare environments.

---

## Conclusion

**CarePath Journey turns a fragmented healthcare experience into a clear, connected, and trackable patient journey.**

By bringing documents, treatment progress, requirements, notifications, and caregiver coordination together, the platform demonstrates how a patient-centric digital layer can make healthcare journeys easier to understand and manage.
